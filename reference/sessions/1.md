# Session 1

## The commune

- We live in a commune with about 20 other people.
  - We've all lived here a while, at least 10 years each.
- The archdruid keeps the settlement safe.
  - He has set up totems around our clearing: anyone who approaches sees an illusion of a rock face and goes around.
  - Our guards dissuade people from climbing if it looks like someone is going to try.
- It's totally not a cult. It's a commune that prays to Freya, primarily.
- On this continent there are no divisions based on religion; conflict here is based more on geography.
- The commune as a whole follows a trinity of deities:
  - Lunareti, god of night and sea
  - Celestia, the sun goddess
  - Freya, goddess of nature. Her work is what we see most, hence we pray to her most.

## Dion

- We are dedicated to the child Dion, a child of Freya and her messiah.
  - Dion is the Meruion. He will ascend when he is 19; he is currently 10.
  - He's not the messiah, he's a very naughty boy.
  - He's a half-elf mixed with halfling, with unique features.
- Allister, the archdruid, is Dion's teacher.
- We've all been here since before Dion came, and we all helped raise him.

## The attack

- Allister left earlier that day with Po, and came back early with a masked man.
  - There are 10 beasts with him (gnolls).
  - The man's mask has a white skull on it, and the same brand is on the beasts.
- The masked man tells us to give him the boy, and throws Po's severed head into the village.
- We are clearly going to get wrecked, but we try to save the boy.
- Oh no, Allister is dead.
- The masked man dimension-doors into the log house and grabs Dion from the cooking pot we hid him in, under a nice thick chowder.
- Botox tries to pry Dion away from him with his shovel, rolls a 1, and cracks Dion on the head.
- The masked man dimension-doors away with Dion.
  - Lunalia follows and tries to save Dion with Thorn Whip. She frees him, but maims his leg.
  - Another gnoll picks Dion up, and the masked man taunts Lunalia as he and 5 of the gnolls flee.
- The other 5 gnolls stay back to fight. We eventually kill them, but the masked man gets away with Dion.
