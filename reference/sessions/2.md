# Session 2

## After the attack

- Wait, we kept one gnoll prisoner.
- Everyone is shell-shocked. Most have never seen this kind of brutality, including many of the party.
- Botox tries to intimidate the surviving gnoll. So does Storvald, but he crit-fails, and the gnoll pulls Storvald's greataxe to his own throat and slits it. Metal as hell.
- Six villagers survive: a mother and child, a couple of grown men and a woman.
- Storvald grabs a knife and a short sword and starts skinning gnolls.
- Olde wants to leave right away to track the masked man and find Dion, but the rest of the party is hesitant.
- We know the contingency if the Meruion or the archdruid are interfered with:
  - Go to the temple of Freya and tell them what has happened; the temple will guide us.
  - We are life-bound to the Meruion until he reaches ascendancy.
- Garth checks Allister's body: very nice vestments, a holy symbol of Freya, a small, beautifully ornate dagger, and a letter.
- The letter says: if anything happens to me, travel north until you meet the Emerald River, follow it north-east to the Cerulean Lake, and you'll find the temple of Freya near the Emerald Citadel. Inform the high priest of Freya.
- We debate. Some want to go to the citadel; others fear that would take too long and that we must find Dion first.
  - The village as a whole votes to go to the citadel, 9 to 5, with the villagers split 3 to 3.
- We search around. Olde tries to search Po's body, but we only have his head.

## To the Emerald Citadel

- We set off the next morning. We eat poorly on the third night.
- We meet the river and follow it north-east, and eventually break free of the forest.
  - We see a huge expanse of water; we can't see the other side.
  - Eventually we see a small spire rising out of what looks like land in the middle of the lake.
  - Snow-capped mountains are visible in the distance.
- We follow the lake around until we come to farming communities, and encourage the villagers to go to the farmers for aid. After some odd interactions we mostly all head to the citadel.
- The lake seems to wrap around the citadel: it sits out in the middle of the lake on a peninsula that slopes upward, with sheer cliffs at the end and the citadel on top.
- The gates are open. The city is beautifully crafted: white marble walls, green, white and silver spires, like an elven metropolis.
- It's very busy, with lots of people about, many holy symbols of Freya on people and buildings, forges, guards patrolling the streets, and barracks.

## The temple of Freya

- We reach the temple at the end of the city. It seems open to the community, and there are people in vestments like Allister's.
- One of them sees us and is immediately, deeply concerned. We tell him of Dion's kidnapping and Allister's death, and he fetches more robed men with more adornments.
- We enter a great sanctum with a tree of life set into the floor in honour of Freya.
  - A very old half-elf sits on a throne, and people are lined up to receive his blessing. He is the current Meruion; some of us know Dion is to replace him when he comes of age, and the old man is supposed to pass on his power.
  - Beyond the throne is a glowing green crystalline female figure, suspended in the air.
- We are taken to the back and seated at a big table. The three high-ranking men sit at one end; the rest of us, civilians included, are invited to join.
- They check on the commune every day "through the power of Freya", and had already discovered we were gone.
- We tell them everything and describe the masked man and the gnolls.
- One of the civilians is disappointed we didn't go after Dion. Botox tells him off, politely.
- They will send citadel guards out to find Dion and the gnolls. Dorlan tries to get them to send us instead, in exchange for food and drink.
- They agree we can go with the guards in the morning. They will house us, since our homes were destroyed, and give us work.
- We may take armour and weapons. We have a great feast that evening.
- We meet Captain Callan and three guards, Quid, Gray and Sky, who will travel with us.
- Botox suspiciously goes to pray in front of the CRYSTAL WOMAN.

## Back to the commune

- Callan wakes us the next day. We're taken to the bottom of the tower, fed well, and given rations.
- The plan: go back to the commune first and search from there. The other commune members will be put to work in the citadel.
- We travel 7 days back with no issues, and hunt well.
- The stone illusion has gone, since no one was there to charge the totems.
- On the way, the guards told us the commune has been compromised and has to be destroyed: we have to burn our own village.
  - Golgamutt blesses the buildings while Clover plays "Angel" by Sarah McLachlan.

## The gnoll cave

- We pick up the gnolls' trail, with blood on it, likely Dion's from his wounds.
- Josh rolls a 1 on a Perception check; some of us do passably. Fugly stops us ahead of a clearing: three humanoid creatures in front of a cave opening.
- We try to sneak forward. Most of us are bad at it, the guards are REALLY bad at it, and Olde rolled a nat 20 but didn't tell anyone.
- The gnolls see us coming. Roll initiative!
  - Captain Callan is a badass; his guards are a mixed bag.
  - Golgamutt Eldritch Blasts Storvald in the backside.
  - Olde lands consecutive crits on one gnoll.
  - Clover tries to convince a gnoll his shoes are tied together. The gnoll knows full well he isn't wearing shoes, and the guards kill him; he dies thinking Clover is an idiot.
  - Clover critically heals Storvald, though.
  - Storvald crits and cleaves the last gnoll somewhere very painful.
- Combat at the cave mouth is over, but one gnoll blew a horn into the cave, so others may be alerted.
