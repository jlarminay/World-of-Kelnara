# Session 4

## Back at the Emerald Citadel

- We head back to the Emerald Citadel: the elegance of an elven city crossed with the massive power of dwarven architecture.
- Callan leads us in, greets the priests, and says we need to meet the high priests.
- We enter the sanctuary, with everything as before: the symbol of Freya, the current Meruion, the floating crystalline female figure.
- We're taken to the back room with the long table again. Large windows overlook the Cerulean Lake.
  - We meet the high priests, who are all elven; most of the priests and leaders seem to be elven.
- We tell them what we did, and that we feel Dion isn't in immediate danger.
- We mention Xezus, and they are quite taken aback, surprised to hear of another deity. We explain what the masked man is teaching, and that he seems to hail from the northern mountains.
- They will send more seasoned scouts to search the northern mountains, and then involve us in Dion's retrieval.
  - Botox objects, but the high priests insist we leave it to them for now: the mountains are incredibly perilous and will test even their most experienced warriors.
- In the meantime they give us jobs in the citadel based on our skills:
  - The bard becomes an entertainer.
  - The barbarians join the citadel guard in the barracks.
  - The warlocks are assigned to the library, as is Garth the wizard (though he's a bit more of a machine… as is Botox).
  - Olde the ranger becomes a scout.
  - The druids work in the temple.
- We have renown in our faction!
- At a tavern to decompress, Botox says something feels wrong and we should be out finding Dion. Clover and Dorlan tell him to relax; Storvald agrees a bit with both, and thinks we should just stay sceptical.

## What we learned

- The Tree of Life is this world's genesis. The gods made the tree, and it has spawned everything known to exist.
- All creatures in the books are "Sentients". The elves were the first, and the dragonborn are the youngest.
  - In this world, what prejudice exists is mostly aimed at dragonborn, because they are linked to the destructive dragons.
- The chromatic dragons were the first to spring from the tree. They were destructive, and were going to annihilate the world.
- The tree spat out other species to slow them down. One was the giants, now believed gone after losing a war against the dragons.
- The last thing the tree spat out was the metallic dragons.
- The chromatic dragons scorched the tree to stop it creating more life. This made the Scorched Lands. No new life has come from the tree in thousands of years.
- The metallic dragons were still losing to the chromatic dragons, so the gods raised a champion in each of the realms. The champions took the chromatic dragons to the Scorched Lands and sealed them in the abyss.
- This took so much magic that the champions crystallised. They were the original Meruions.
- The current Meruions have to go and re-seal the chromatic dragons. Our current Meruion is VERY old, so we need Dion back badly: he is being raised and taught to go with the other two Meruions to renew the seal.
- Because of all this, religious faith is strong: there is tangible evidence in the world of what the gods did.

## Three years pass

- We live in the citadel for three years, and our renown grows.
  - Clover becomes very popular, develops a bad drug problem, and blows through 86% of her gold.
  - Golgamutt becomes a researcher at the grand library and writes a massive best seller, *A Unique Perspective on Things That Already Happened*. It pays for a nice house in The Heights, which his trusted assistants watch while he travels.
  - Others live interesting lives too.
- During the three years we hear various reports from the scouts as some return. No sign of Dion.

## The caravan at the Northern Outpost

- After three years we are summoned to the grand temple's war room.
- The priests need our help. A caravan came through the northern outpost and raised concern, so the crew there is detaining it.
  - We are to go and check on the caravan and run an identification check.
  - It's two weeks to get there, and things may be tense: they'll have been detained for two weeks by the time we arrive.
  - One of those detained has been described as resembling the Meruion.
- We are released from our current roles.

## Roslow's Radish Farm

- We travel the main road for a few days and, as it gets dark, come to a smallish farmstead not far from the lake.
- A rough, bearded man in a white tunic, baggy trousers and muddy boots opens the door: Roslow's Radish Farm.
  - He says we can stay. He wants to sell his radishes to the citadel.
  - A couple of us notice the building is very new, while most farms around the city have been there for generations. It's suspicious that this new farm claims to have the "best" radishes, though he seems to be a genuine farmer, and the only one there.
  - Asked whether he farmed elsewhere before, he says yes, totally convincingly (Storvald's Insight: 3).
  - He recognises Golgamutt, knows his book, and asks him to sign his copy.
  - A few of us stay in a spare room; the rest camp by the house.
- After a long rest he wishes us well, tells us to remember Roslow's radishes, and to talk him up in town, especially Golgamutt.

## Green Rock Farm

- Two more days on, we find another farmstead, apparently growing radishes. Fugly and Botox DOUBLE CRIT their Nature checks: we absolutely know those are radishes.
- Outside it are the ruins of what may have been a town, around a hundred years old. The farmstead looks about 20 years old.
- A tall, slender man with blond hair answers, notices we're from the Emerald City, and welcomes us to stay.
  - His name is Jimothy. He also recognises Golgamutt and asks him to sign his copy of the book; he gave another copy to a friend some time ago.
  - He serves food almost exactly like Roslow's meal: similar spices, same preparation, everything.
  - The town used to be called Green Rock. He was raised here, was taken away with some survivors after the village was attacked, and they came back later to start the farm.
  - He was here with a friend named Roslow. He sells radishes to local towns and passing travellers, since they travel well.
  - He says he doesn't know what happened to Roslow, and doesn't want to talk about it.
  - Botox apologises for pressing, and tells him we met Roslow recently. Jimothy asks how Roslow is doing, and seems sad.
- Garth pokes around the house. There are three rooms: one unlived-in with a couple of beds, one well decorated but not lived in recently, and Jimothy's, which is.
  - In Jimothy's room is a crumpled note: "My dearest Roslow, for many years we've built a life, looked out for each other. Something has been missing from our bond…" He loves Roslow.
  - It looks like it was folded once, then crumpled up by whoever read it.

## The black-robed caravan

- Three days later, cresting a hill, we see a caravan pulled by two black horses, with 5 riders on horseback, 4 people walking beside it and 1 driving.
- We don't notice much until they're 100 feet away. They carry no banner, wear black robes, and don't seem heavily armoured. They are exceedingly pale, with skin tones and attire unlike anything familiar.
- One rider pulls ahead and stops the caravan.
  - Storvald notices what may be very old battle stains on the leader's robes; Fugly sees them on everyone.
- The leader greets us in a very friendly manner. He says they've travelled from the north, from Olympus, and passed through the outpost without issue.
  - Storvald challenges this, and Fugly sees through the lies.
- Storvald asks to check out the caravan, and they say sure. Storvald, Botox and Fugly move in to check it, and get some very hard looks as they do…
