# Session 3

## Into the cave

- As we approach the cave, black plumes of smoke pour out, as if a campfire has just been put out with water. Our vision in the cave is completely obscured.
- Botox and Garth walk their construct selves right in: they can't see, but they won't take damage from the smoke.
  - They feel their way along the left wall (the characters' left, not Paul's) and realise they're heading uphill.
  - They get attacked, and flee back through the smoke to the party.
- We decide to wait for the smoke to clear. Olde scouts around, climbs on top of the mound, finds a small hole that is likely a vent, and plugs it. Even more smoke billows out.
- Waiting outside, we hear two short blasts on a horn, and the smoke starts clearing.
- Golgamutt tries to blow the smoke out with Prestidigitation. Two gnolls pop out, surprised.
  - We kill the first. The second pulls out a horn and blows it nine times, then runs back in.
  - Storvald is raging, so he chases him in blindly.
- The gnolls are below us, at the mouth of another tunnel. We fight the first few.
  - One comes out with a bone whip and splits Gray in two, then cracks it down on another guard and splits him in half too.
- Botox and Garth climb up to the boulder trap above the enemies, and Botox crits on pushing the boulders: they crush one gnoll and badly hurt the whip-wielder.
  - They bump chests, and it sounds like two pans clattering together.
- We finish off the gnoll lord, and the last gnoll grovels and surrenders.

## Loot

- Bone whip (2d4, 10 ft reach), taken by Storvald
- Bone javelin, taken by Storvald (same as a regular javelin, but cooler)
- Bone dice, scimitars and bone bows
- Another vial: orange liquid that flickers, with black smoke above it
- Golgamutt and Garth find pouches buried in the gnoll beds, with some gold, silver and copper.

## The bone throne

- Down the hallway is a throne-like chair made of bone. A twine pendant with the symbol of Freya hangs from its arm: Dion had it when he was taken.
- There's no sign of a body, or of Dion; we don't think he was killed here.
- Searching for a door or passage, we find lots of gnoll tracks, plus human tracks near the throne: an adult and a halfling. They stop in front of a wall without going through it. We figure the masked man took Dion through a dimension door.

## The surviving gnoll

- We retcon that Olde would have used Hunter's Mark on the surviving gnoll we seemingly just let leave. He tracks it successfully, and we follow it into the night and camp.
- We are attacked by an owlbear!
  - Olde groggily jumps up with a pipe in his mouth, marks the owlbear, then shoots a crossbow bolt into Storvald's backside. Storvald uses Relentless Endurance to stay up.
  - Callan does big damage to the owlbear; Storvald runs in and whiffs, and the owlbear goes for Callan, the one who's actually a threat.
  - Olde then shoots Callan. Olde doesn't understand that we're fighting the owlbear.
  - Storvald cleaves the owlbear with his greataxe, and Dave perfectly impersonates its beautiful, mournful hoot-growl.
  - Golgamutt hits Storvald with an Eldritch Blast.
  - Callan finishes it off.
- We long rest and level up.
- Next morning we pick up the gnoll's trail again. It's leaving less of a mess, suggesting it's moving much slower. We catch up; it seems to be wandering, not heading anywhere. Olde marks it, and he and Storvald sneak up and easily grapple it. We tie it up and talk.
  - The masked man has disappeared.
  - In hindsight, these gnolls bore the horned skull: the sign of Xezus. "It's our symbol", which seems to include the masked man.
  - The cave was their den; the masked man found them there and taught them about Xezus and their own power. The gnoll doesn't seem to know where he went.
  - The gnoll is from the northern mountains.
  - The masked man taught the lord "fire breathing", which was probably just the fire-breathing potion we now have.
  - The gnoll was heading to the Scorched Lands, where he may have kin. People get exiled there; it's not a nice place. The Tree of Life is thought to be there.
- What we know: the Emerald Citadel is the last big city before the northern mountains. Gold is mined in the eastern part of the northern mountains, in the province of Celestia. We don't know what's in the western part.
- We decide to go back to the Emerald Citadel, since it's on the way north. Callan doubts anything will be in the northern mountains, but says the grand temple should be told.
- After much debate we kill the gnoll: he would spread heresy about another deity.
- Note: the masked man taught the gnolls about Xezus, a deity outside our trinity of Celestia, Lunareti and Freya.
