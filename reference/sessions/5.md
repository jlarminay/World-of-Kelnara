# Session 5

## The fight at the caravan

- As we approach the caravan, some of us hear hushed muttering from inside it. Botox can tell a spell is being cast, and warns everyone.
- Chaos ensues…
  - Fugly motions for someone to cause a distraction, and some of us yell.
  - Olde searches the cart, but sees nothing.
- Dorlan uses Mage Hand to snap the reins, and the cart takes off.
  - Some of us try to jump on it. Clover lands on it.
  - It runs over Curtis, badly.
  - Olde tries to jump on, but hits an invisible wall.
- The cart and two of the riders take off. The four enemies on foot mount the other three horses.
- Storvald cleaves the leg off the horse carrying the leader who first greeted us and two others, and they spill to the ground, prone. He yells at them to yield: he doesn't want to kill them.
- Olde casts Entangle on the ground under the fleeing cart. The horses get out, and the cart sludges through.
- From the top of the cart, Clover casts an illusion of a rock in front of the horses. They fail their save and try to avoid it.
- Then a fight really breaks out.
  - We shoot out a wheel on the cart. It stops, and the driver is thrown… he's not moving.
  - Fugly summons his goat and attacks through it. It caves in a man's face: he now has a reverse goat face.
  - Storvald, who has done nothing wrong to this point, finally joins the REAL fighting. He almost cuts one man in two, while actually cutting another man in two.
  - Clover goes into the cart, and Olde looks in.
  - Olde tries to hide under the cart, and bonks his head like a coconut.
- Clover comes out and yells at everyone to stop fighting. Others come out of the cart too.

## Dion

- Eventually Dion comes out of the cart, and he also tells us to stop fighting.
  - He says he is now where he wants to be.
  - He says there are no gods, and everyone should find power only in themselves.
  - He says we should collect all the Meruions and stop the ritual. Let the strong prevail.
  - He says he has been shown the truth by Xezus.
- We think he has been brainwashed…
- Clover says she is with Dion, but tries to sign to us that something is off and we should investigate. Her hand gestures are TERRIBLE, and Dion calls her out.
- Clover tries to approach Dion, but the masked man steps out of the cart.
- Dion says we could go north and find Xezus ourselves, or travel with him.
- The masked man is Valron. Once he is named he takes off his mask: he is an aasimar.

## Travelling with the cult

- We debate what to do, and eventually decide to head back with them, at least as far as Green Rock.
- Olde debates with Valron.
  - Valron doesn't accept that we wouldn't go and warn the Emerald Citadel if we travelled with them for only three days.
  - Olde says he won't leave Dion, so we must travel with them for more than three days.
  - Valron says something to the other men. Olde gets it out of Dion that they were told to keep an eye on us.
- A cultist threatens Olde. Olde uses Disguise Self to look like the cultist, and threatens him back in a mocking imitation of his voice. The cultist is shattered.
- We travel with them for two days. We have one more night before we reach Green Rock.
- We assume this is the caravan the Northern Outpost was detaining. Since it got away, we assume the outpost is no longer viable: everyone there is probably dead, or the whole outpost destroyed.

## What Dion told us

- At first he was very scared after being kidnapped.
- They healed his leg with magic he had never seen before, and he has seen tons of magic like never before.
- Until now, magic has been cast in the colour of our goddess: Freya's is green and silver. Dion has seen magic of many colours.
  - He cast Goodberry in a beautiful red, which we know is his favourite colour.
  - He says he now casts magic through himself, no longer from Freya.
- Xezus is not a god. He is a creature as old as the dragons, unlike anything before and not in any traditional teachings: a foundation-shaking entity of fire and earth, from the era of dragons, who can share endless wisdom from his time here.
- Past the Northern Outpost there is a secret path north, through the forest and into the mountains, that leads to him.

## The cultists

- They don't appear crazy. They have all met Xezus. They are hardened from living in the mountains, and seem clear-headed in their beliefs.
- One of them told us:
  - The whole trinity is false. There are no gods; we were lied to.
  - The three churches are wrong, and the exiling is a terrible crime.
  - The dragons are real and the seals are real, but people other than the Meruions can perform the seal: anybody can use magic, and can perform the seals with training.
  - The trinity is keeping people weak and trapped.
  - They want to spread the word by getting the Meruions, showing them the truth of the world, and opening their eyes.
- The cultists believe the Meruions could create a new seal with their power from within, without relying on magic from the deities.
- We eventually realise Dion is wearing a pendant with divination magic. It is likely blocking the Emerald Citadel from tracking him, shielding him from detection.

## Golgamutt's past

- Golgamutt was a deckhand on a ship that sank in the southern sea, below Discoverer's Bay, by the Sunken City.
- There he saw a sunken city built in an architecture never seen before.
- He has since done a great deal of research looking for anything about these ruins. No books or research papers exist on the matter, and nobody seems to know anything.

## New places

- The northern lake west of the Emerald Citadel is called Death's Lake.
- The more southern lake is called Glacier Falls.
- Two port cities: Dollingr Port and Unity Port.
- A road leads from Green Rock Farm to Unity Port.
